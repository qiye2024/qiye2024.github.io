At the Ocean Carbon & Biogeochemistry (OCB) Program’s Benthic Ecosystem and Carbon Synthesis (BECS) Workshop held at Princeton University, Qi delivered a 45-minute tutorial on running the Carbon, Benthic Ecosystems, and Diagenesis model (CBED model, developed by Dr. Subhadeep Rakshit from Princeton University) in R/RStudio.

During the session, Qi guided participants through the full modeling workflow, from setting up simulations and selecting global ocean sites, to visualizing sediment nutrient depth profiles, top boundary exchange conditions, and bottom nutrient flux time series. The tutorial aimed to help both modelers and observationalists better integrate benthic biogeochemistry into coastal and global carbon cycle studies.

The workshop fostered dynamic discussions among scientists from diverse institutions, highlighting the importance of reproducible modeling tools and cross-disciplinary collaboration in advancing our understanding of benthic–pelagic interactions and ocean carbon dynamics. 🌊
